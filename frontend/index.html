Trend Detection Approach

The system compares a simple weight-threshold baseline with an EMA-based trend summariser.

Baseline: Alert when Current Weight - Baseline Weight > 0.5 kg

EMA: Exponential Moving Average is used to smooth noisy weight readings and identify persistent trends.

The summariser also considers breathlessness, swelling, and fatigue before assigning LOW, MEDIUM, or HIGH priority.

Human-in-the-Loop Safety

This system is a decision-support prototype. It does not diagnose heart failure, prescribe medication, change treatment, or replace clinicians.

High-priority outputs are intended for human clinical review.

Evaluation Limitation

The dataset and escalation outcomes are synthetic benchmark data.

Therefore, the reported EMA metrics should not be interpreted as real-world clinical accuracy.

Real-world validation would require real patient data, clinician-defined ground truth, larger datasets, and clinical evaluation.
