# Patient Journeys

## Overview

The prototype uses synthetic patient journeys to test how the Trend Summariser behaves for different levels of urgency.

The dataset contains 30 simulated patient profiles covering stable patients, one-time weight spikes, persistent increasing trends, symptom-only cases, and noisy or incomplete reporting.

The journeys are designed for testing and evaluation only. They are not real patient records or clinical evidence.

---

## Journey 1 — Stable Patient

### Patient

P001

### Scenario

The patient records relatively stable home measurements across multiple days.

### Observed Pattern

- Weight remains close to baseline.
- No persistent increasing trend is detected.
- No significant symptom combination is reported.

### System Output

Priority:

LOW

Trend:

No meaningful EMA trend

### Human Review

No high-priority escalation is generated by the prototype.

The care team can continue normal monitoring according to their workflow.

---

## Journey 2 — Persistent Increasing Trend

### Patient

P017

### Scenario

The patient's weight increases gradually across multiple reporting days.

### Observed Pattern

- Weight increases over several days.
- EMA smoothing confirms a persistent increasing trend.
- Symptoms such as breathlessness, swelling, and fatigue are reported.

### System Output

Priority:

HIGH

Trend:

Persistent increasing EMA trend

### Human Review Point

A care team member reviews the recent weight trend and reported symptoms.

The system recommends human clinical review.

The system does not diagnose the patient or recommend treatment.

---

## Journey 3 — Symptoms Without Major Weight Increase

### Patient

P025

### Scenario

The patient reports symptoms even though the weight change is relatively small.

### Observed Pattern

- No major EMA weight increase.
- Symptoms are present.
- Symptom information contributes to the priority score.

### System Output

Priority:

HIGH

Trend:

No meaningful EMA weight trend

### Human Review Point

The care team reviews the reported symptoms and available home-monitoring information.

This demonstrates that the prototype does not rely only on weight thresholds.

---

## Journey 4 — One-Time Weight Spike

### Patient

P009

### Scenario

The patient has a temporary increase in one individual weight reading.

### Observed Pattern

- A single reading crosses the simple baseline threshold.
- The increase does not form a persistent multi-day trend.

### System Output

Trend model:

No persistent escalation

### Human Review

The case demonstrates how a trend-based approach can avoid treating every individual abnormal reading as a persistent escalation.

The final interpretation remains with the care team.

---

## Journey 5 — Missing Reporting Days

### Patient

P028

### Scenario

The patient does not report measurements on every expected day.

### Observed Pattern

Missing reporting days include:

- Day 3
- Day 5

### System Behaviour

The system identifies the incomplete reporting pattern during edge-case testing.

The patient profile is still processed without treating missing data as a clinical diagnosis.

### Human Review

Missing or incomplete data should be considered by the care team when interpreting the summarised trend.

---

## Journey 6 — Noisy Sensor Readings

### Patient

P029

### Scenario

The patient's readings contain measurement variation.

### Observed Pattern

- Weight readings show sensor-level variation.
- Reporting is also incomplete.
- The profile is processed by the trend summariser.

### System Behaviour

EMA smoothing helps reduce the effect of individual reading fluctuations.

The prototype does not automatically convert noisy readings into a clinical decision.

## Human-in-the-Loop Decision Points

### Decision Point 1

The Trend Summariser identifies a potentially meaningful pattern.

↓

### Decision Point 2

The Care Team Dashboard presents the summarised trend and symptoms.

↓

### Decision Point 3

A human care team member reviews the available information.

↓

### Decision Point 4

The care team decides whether further clinical action is appropriate.

The prototype does not make autonomous clinical or treatment decisions.

---

## Safety Boundary

All patient journeys in this project are synthetic.

The Trend Summariser is a decision-support prototype intended to prioritise meaningful changes and reduce low-value alert overload.

It is not a diagnostic system and must not replace professional clinical judgement.
