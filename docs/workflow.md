# Human Review Workflow

## Workflow

Patient

↓

Home readings and symptom reports collected

↓

Trend Summariser

↓

EMA-based trend and symptom analysis

↓

Priority assigned

↓

Care Team Dashboard

↓

Human clinical review

↓

Care team decides next appropriate action

---

## System Role

The Trend Summariser helps the care team identify meaningful changes from repeated home readings.

The system analyses:

- Weight changes from baseline
- EMA-based weight trends
- Increasing trend patterns
- Breathlessness
- Swelling
- Fatigue

The system does not:

- Diagnose heart failure
- Prescribe medication
- Change treatment
- Make autonomous clinical decisions

---

## Human Review Points

### Review Point 1 — High Priority Alert

When a patient is classified as HIGH priority, a care team member reviews the patient's recent trend and reported symptoms.

The dashboard provides the summarised information to support human review.

### Review Point 2 — Before Any Clinical Action

The care team reviews the available patient information before deciding whether further clinical action is needed.

The final decision remains with the care team.

## Example — Persistent Increasing Trend

Patient:

P017

Weight trend:

68.0 kg → 68.4 kg → 68.8 kg → 69.3 kg → 69.8 kg → 70.2 kg → 70.6 kg

The EMA trend shows a persistent increase over multiple observations.

Symptoms:

Breathlessness + Swelling + Fatigue

System output:

HIGH priority

Trend:

Persistent increasing EMA trend

Next step:

Human clinical review recommended.

The Trend Summariser does not make a diagnosis or treatment decision.

The final decision remains with the care team.

---

## Review 2 Safety Boundary

The prototype is designed as a decision-support system.

Its purpose is to reduce low-value alert overload and highlight meaningful patterns for human review.

All clinical decisions remain under human supervision.
