# Human Review Workflow

## Workflow

Patient
↓
Home readings collected
↓
Trend Summariser
↓
Pattern and symptom analysis
↓
Priority assigned
↓
Care Team Dashboard
↓
Human clinical review
↓
Care team decides next appropriate action

## System Role

The Trend Summariser helps the care team identify
meaningful changes from repeated home readings.

The system does not:

- Diagnose heart failure
- Prescribe medication
- Change treatment
- Make autonomous clinical decisions

## Human Review Points

### Review Point 1 — High Priority Alert

When a patient is classified as HIGH priority,
a care team member reviews the patient's recent trend
and reported symptoms.

### Review Point 2 — Before Any Clinical Action

The care team reviews the available information
before deciding whether further clinical action is needed.

## Example

P002:

Weight trend:
68.0 kg → 68.8 kg → 69.6 kg → 70.4 kg

Symptoms:
Breathlessness + Swelling + Fatigue

System output:
HIGH priority

Next step:
Human clinical review recommended.

The final decision remains with the care team.
